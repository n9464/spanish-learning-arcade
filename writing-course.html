Step 1

Clear sentence foundations

Core sentence rule

A strong sentence starts with a clear subject, a correctly formed verb, and information that actually belongs together.

Writing priority

Accuracy comes before style. Clear verb tense and agreement matter more than trying to sound advanced too early.

Leo. basic but incomplete as writing
Leo un artículo. clearer object
Leo un artículo por la noche. time detail
Leo un artículo por la noche porque quiero aprender más. reason + fuller idea

Step 4

Paragraph structure

1. Topic sentence State the main idea clearly.
2. Support Add a reason, detail, or explanation.
3. Example Make it concrete.
4. Closing Finish or summarize the idea.

Step 5

Formal vs informal tone

Informal

Hola, profe.

¿Me ayudas con la tarea?

Nos vemos.

Formal

Buenas tardes, profesora.

Quisiera pedirle ayuda con la tarea.

Muchas gracias por su tiempo.

Step 6

Accuracy lab: tense and agreement

Check agreement

Las clases son interesantes. plural noun + plural adjective

Step 7

Guided writing workshop

Before you submit

Use the scaffold first. Then check verb tense, agreement, connector choice, and whether the tone matches the situation.

Step 8

Mixed mastery check

Most common writing mistakes

  • Changing tense without noticing.
  • Using a connector that does not match the logic.
  • Writing an adjective that does not agree with the noun.
  • Using an overly informal tone in a formal message.
  • Adding details without a clear topic sentence.
