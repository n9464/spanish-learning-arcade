Step 1

How this vocabulary system works

Themes

You learn words inside real domains like food, school, emotions, and travel so the meaning stays tied to a situation.

Frequency

The most useful everyday words come first. Rare vocabulary waits until the core system is already stable.

Word family example

hablar hablador hablado

One root can unlock many related words faster than isolated memorization.

Collocation example

tener + hambre

Words become easier to use when you learn their natural partners.

Step 8

Adaptive review lab

How review priority is chosen

New words start at the top. Then the system favors low-accuracy words, low-streak words, and words you keep missing in context.

Most common vocabulary mistakes

  • Memorizing a translation but not the natural collocation.
  • Knowing a root word but not recognizing its family members.
  • Remembering a word in isolation but failing to use it in a sentence.
  • Reviewing only easy words instead of the weak ones.
